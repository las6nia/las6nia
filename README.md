## A little about me

Hey, I'm Luis!

I'm studying **Computer Science at UW–Madison**, exploring the space between software development, IT, and cybersecurity.

What started as curiosity about computers has turned into a growing interest in understanding how everything connects — from the code behind a website to the operating systems and networks running underneath it.

I enjoy learning by building, troubleshooting, and occasionally breaking something just to figure out how to fix it.

A few things about me:

- Based in Wisconsin, studying Computer Science.
- Bilingual in English and Spanish.
- Interested in both software engineering and hands-on IT.
- Enjoy working on websites and technology projects for my community.
- Currently exploring Linux, networking, databases, and backend development.
- Outside of tech, I'm usually enjoying good food or spending time with my dog.

> I don't know everything, but I enjoy figuring things out.

## More than just writing code

I think one of the most interesting parts of technology is discovering what happens behind the scenes.

I enjoy exploring different areas rather than limiting myself to one programming language or framework.

That includes:

- **Software:** Building applications and improving my problem-solving skills.
- **Infrastructure:** Learning how operating systems, servers, and networks work.
- **Community:** Helping organizations and churches with websites and technology.
- **Experimentation:** Trying unfamiliar tools, setting up virtual machines, and learning from mistakes.

My long-term goal is to keep developing the skills to create useful software while understanding the systems that make it possible.

## A few things I believe

**Stay curious.** There's always something new to understand.

**Keep improving.** Being a beginner at something is temporary if you keep practicing.
